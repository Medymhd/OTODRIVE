01 / Our services

Four services.
One standard.

Autogas installation, LPG fueling, diagnosis and system checks. The service you need depends on the vehicle and the stage it is at — the team will tell you what is required before any work begins.

01

LPG fueling

We offer LPG refueling and a customer loyalty program. Our LPG is competitively priced and ensures you save up to 40% over regular petrol. We enjoy seeing customers benefit from our excellent services provided as you refuel, such as ensuring your car is operating at peak performance by checking your fluid levels and clearing your view by washing your windscreen. Our loyalty program enables clients to get rewards from fueling with us and the testimonies speak for themselves. Here, we put a lot of effort into ensuring you get the best out of our services.

02

Autogas system installation

We install Autogas systems manufactured by the best manufacturers in the world. In partnership with global leading providers such as Westport Fuels (Italy) and LPGTech (Poland) of Autogas Systems, we are licensed by KEBS to convert gasoline-powered vehicles to run on LPG under our brand, OTODRIVE. We have the best technical team that will ensure your vehicle will attain the best mileage while using the Autogas system and enjoy the benefits of having a hybrid fueling system.

03

Free car diagnosis

Here at Otodrive, we believe the customer should enjoy their new vehicle just as you would your mobile electronic devices. We realised it’s unfair to charge a client on tasks such as diagnosis since it’s their way of analysing how well their technology works and to find out if there’s any issues with their Autogas system. We offer free vehicle diagnosis and ensure the customer uses their vehicle in its best condition.

04

Free LPG system check and service

When in doubt, come get your kit checked out! We offer free LPG system checks for all Autogas vehicles. Our company is dedicated to ensuring all Autogas vehicles are safe on the roads. Traveling in a healthy car, with cheap fuel and a carefree mood, is what we aim for. We offer free LPG system checks to ensure you can always know the condition of your vehicle and take the appropriate action when needed.
