01 / Owners

What changed
after the switch.

These are the owners’ words, from the first conversion to the everyday refill. The savings and the driving experience come up again and again.

“Switching to LPG has been a game-changer for me! Not only is the fuel cost lower, but I also feel good about reducing my carbon footprint. My car runs just as smoothly, and I’m saving a significant amount each month on fuel expenses. It’s great to know I’m doing something for the environment too!”

Jane M. — Nairobi

“At first, I was skeptical about converting my car to LPG, but now I can’t imagine going back! LPG is way more economical, and I’ve cut my fuel costs by almost 40%. The conversion process was smooth, and I’ve noticed that my engine even runs quieter. I highly recommend it to anyone considering the switch.”

Chris O. — Nairobi

“The conversion to LPG has helped me enjoy driving without worrying about the cost. I was unsure at first, but the savings have been amazing. Plus, I feel great about using a cleaner fuel. My car runs beautifully, and I love knowing I’m making a sustainable choice!”

Violet T. — Athi River

“LPG conversion was honestly the best decision for my car. It’s so affordable compared to petrol, and I don’t feel any difference in performance. I used to spend a lot on fuel each month, but now I can put that extra money into other things. It’s a win-win for me!”

Ahmed R. — Kitengela

“Going LPG was the right choice. With the rising costs of petrol, I wanted a solution that would be both economical and reliable. The savings have been real! I can drive more freely without worrying about fuel prices as much. It’s efficient, smooth, and surprisingly easy to maintain.”

Lucy N. — Thika

“After converting to LPG, I’ve noticed a big difference in my wallet! I’m saving on fuel without compromising performance. The car feels just as powerful, and the conversion was easier than I expected. It’s amazing how much cleaner it is, too. I’d encourage anyone thinking about it to make the switch.”

John K. — Nairobi

Testimonials are published as supplied by Otodrive customers. Individual savings depend on the vehicle, route and fuel prices.
