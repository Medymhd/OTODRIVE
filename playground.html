01 / Why Otodrive

Why
Otodrive?

Our objective is simple — cut what you spend on fuel. Convert your petrol vehicle to LPG and save 40% or more, depending on fuel prices.

Otodrive is an authorized Autogas system installer in Kenya. We are an innovative solutions leader, leveraging world-class technology to deliver superior safety, high quality, and operational excellence for motorists across the country. By converting your gasoline-powered vehicle to a smart, bi-fuel LPG system, we help you save up to 40% on fuel costs while enjoying peak engine performance. We offer high quality, affordable Autogas products with warranty. Otodrive is distinguished by a combination of innovative thought and continuous improvement of automobile technology.

We are KS compliant, have a state-of-the-art workshop and our engineers are certified. In partnership with global leading providers — Westport Fuels (Italy) with Zavoli systems, and Clean Enviro Fuels with Lowgas systems — we are licensed by KEBS to convert gasoline-powered vehicles to run on LPG under our brand, OTODRIVE. Our product conforms to Energy Policy and Regulatory Authority, Kenya Bureau of Standards, and National Environmental Management Authority requirements.
