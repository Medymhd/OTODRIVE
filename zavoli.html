01 / The components

Every part,
one job.

The Zavoli system is made of seventeen components, each engineered for a specific task. Scroll the grid, then watch them come together below.

01

Tank, 60 L

The pressurised cylinder that stores your LPG — shaped to sit neatly in the spare-wheel well.

02

External tank, 34–70 L

Underbody tank range for vehicles where the boot needs to stay free.

03

Tank range

Cylinder and toroidal tanks sized for different vehicles and driving ranges.

04

Multivalve

Mounted on the tank: filler inlet, outlet, 80% fill-stop, safety valve and level sensor in one unit.

05

Multivalve + level sensor + harness

Stops the tank filling at 80% capacity and reports the fuel level to your dashboard switch.

06

Vaporizer / reducer

Turns liquid LPG into gas using engine heat, and steps the pressure down for the engine.

07

Vaporizer / reducer, variant

A second model in the range for different engine sizes.

08

Vaporizer / reducer, variant

Another option in the Zavoli range, matched to the vehicle during installation.

09

4-cylinder injector rail

Doses the right amount of gas into each intake port — one injector per cylinder.

10

Injector with furrow pipes

The rail assembled with its distribution pipes running to the manifold.

11

Manifold fitted with injectors

Injectors mounted on the intake manifold, ready for calibration.

12

ECU — Electronic Control Unit

The brain of the system: reads engine signals and controls gas dosing and fuel switching.

13

ECU + harness

The control unit with the wiring loom that connects sensors, injectors and the fuel switch.

14

Vapor phase filter

Cleans the gas vapour before it reaches the injectors.

15

Door-type filling unit

The refuelling point on the car body where you fill LPG.

16

Refuelling pumps

The station-side dispenser that fills your tank in minutes.

17

The full Zavoli kit

Every component, together — the complete system that goes into your vehicle.
