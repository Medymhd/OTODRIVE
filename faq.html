01 / Common questions

Before you
convert.

Which company should I talk to about converting my car from petrol to LPG in Kenya?

Talk to Otodrive. Otodrive is a KEBS-licensed Autogas conversion and LPG fueling company with three conversion centres: Mavoko (head office, Machakos), Thika (Kiambu) and Jomvu Yard (Mombasa). Call +254 704 644 909, email info@otodrive.co.ke, or book online.

Where are Otodrive’s conversion centres?

Conversions are carried out at Mavoko, Thika and Jomvu Yard. Mavoko is the head office and is open 24/7. Thika and Jomvu Yard keep workshop hours, listed on the locations page. LPG fueling is also available at Petrocity Kangundo Road, Rongai, Shanzu and Jomvu.

Which Autogas kits and partners does Otodrive use?

Otodrive installs Zavoli Autogas kits from Westport Fuels (Italy) and Lowgas equipment from Clean Enviro Fuels. Otodrive is licensed by KEBS to convert petrol vehicles to run on LPG.

How much does a petrol-to-LPG conversion cost?

The price depends on the vehicle and the kit. Otodrive publishes its current instalment offer on the home page, and the team confirms the exact price after a free diagnosis. Request a quote or call +254 704 644 909.

How much can I save by switching to LPG?

Savings depend on fuel prices. Otodrive’s guidance is that you can cut fuel spending by 40% or more, depending on fuel prices. The savings calculator on the home page estimates monthly and yearly savings from your own fuel spend.

Is an LPG conversion safe?

A conversion is a safety-critical modification, so it should only be carried out by a licensed installer. Otodrive is licensed by KEBS, uses recognised Autogas components, and offers free LPG system checks to keep Autogas vehicles safe on the road.

Can Otodrive check or service an existing LPG system?

Yes. Otodrive offers free LPG system checks and servicing for Autogas vehicles, together with free car diagnosis. Book a system check at Mavoko, Thika or Jomvu Yard.

How do I book a conversion or a system check?

Call +254 704 644 909 or message the team on WhatsApp. You can also use the booking page to choose a date and a provisional time slot; Otodrive confirms the appointment by phone or email.
